# Hackathon Speaker Script — Slides 1–4 Handoff

**Speaker:** Rizky  
**Handoff:** Citra begins the live demo on slide 4  
**Target time for this section:** 1:50

Everything inside a blockquote is spoken. Text in brackets is a stage cue and
is not read aloud.

## Slide 1 — Mayarin · 0:20

`[Stand still. Look at the judges before speaking.]`

> Good morning. We are Mayarin.
>
> Accepting crypto is easy. Settling it correctly is hard. We believe merchants
> should be able to accept crypto without becoming crypto companies. They keep
> pricing locally, customers pay with a supported crypto asset, and merchants
> receive the any stablecoin they choose.

`[Pause. Advance to slide 2.]`

## Slide 2 — The settlement gap · 0:40

> Imagine a customer holding ETH or any crypto currency, a store pricing in local fiat currency, and a
> merchant who only wants USDC and want product expansion to various countries. Today, somebody has to close that gap manually.
>
> The customer hunts for the right asset. The merchant has to manage wallets,
> exchange rates, swaps, gas, and reconciliation. And the developer ends up
> rebuilding a payment state machine for every integration.
>
> For us, a checkout is not complete when crypto simply arrives. It is complete
> when the merchant receives the correct settlement and can prove it.

`[Pause briefly on “prove it.” Advance to slide 3.]`

## Slide 3 — One clearing layer · 0:35

> Mayarin is the merchant-grade clearing layer behind the checkout.
>
> The merchant sets a price in the currency they understand and chooses how
> they want to settle. The customer pays with a supported crypto asset through
> a wallet or a plain transfer.
>
> Behind the scenes, Mayarin quotes, locks, converts, settles, and records the
> payment. The customer gets flexibility, while the merchant receives the
> stablecoin they selected with an auditable payment record.

`[Advance to slide 4. Turn slightly toward Citra.]`

## Slide 4 — Live payment demo and handoff · 0:15

> Let us make that real. Parahyangan Supply, an apparel brand in Bandung, sells
> the Rinjani Cargo Pants for IDR 299,000, about twenty-five Singapore dollars.
> The customer will pay with testnet ETH, while the merchant settles in
> USDC—and we will verify that the chain and ledger agree.
>
> Citra, please take us through the real payment showcase.
