## Buyer protection

With buyer protection on, a payment is held in an escrow instead of going straight to you. Turn it on in **Settings → Buyer protection** and set the hold in days (1 to 90).

- The buyer can release the funds to you at any time.
- Nobody disputed by the end of the hold? Mayarin releases the funds to you automatically, usually within about a minute.
- **You can refund the buyer at any time.**

The buyer opts in by connecting a wallet. A buyer who pays from an exchange is paid to you directly, as usual.

## When a buyer opens a dispute

The release stops and the case appears under **Disputes** in the sidebar (needs `payments:read`), with an open count beside it.

1. Open the case and read the buyer's reason in the thread.
2. **Reply** with evidence of delivery, or **refund** the buyer.
3. The arbiter, **Mayarin Resolution**, may ask for more.
4. The funds move: released to you, refunded, or split.

**Answer promptly.** If nobody decides by the deadline, the buyer is refunded. A dispute you ignore is one you lose.

You are told by webhook at each step: `escrow.disputed`, `escrow.dispute.message`, `escrow.dispute.resolved`.

## Refunds outside buyer protection

Refunds work on payments made by deposit. Contract-path refunds are not generally available yet. Contact [support](/contact) with the payment id if you need one.
