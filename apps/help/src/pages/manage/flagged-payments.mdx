---
title: Flagged payments
description: "What it means when Mayarin flags a payment, and what you should do."
---

Mayarin checks every payment when the buyer confirms it.

| Outcome   | What happens                                                          |
| --------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Clear     | Nothing. Most payments.                                               |
| `REVIEW`  | The payment goes ahead. You are told, with the reason.                |
| `BLOCKED` | The payment is refused. The buyer sees only "This payment cannot be processed". |

A `REVIEW` is a heads-up, not a rejection. The rules look for patterns such as many attempts from one address, many attempts on your account in a short window, or an amount far above your usual largest payment.

## What to do

- Look at the payment and the reason before you ship goods.
- A large first-time order flagged as an amount spike is often legitimate. Confirm with the buyer if unsure.
- `BLOCKED` payments are not created, so you have nothing to refund.

You receive a `payment.flagged` [webhook](/integrate/webhooks) for both outcomes. Buyers are never told which rule fired.
