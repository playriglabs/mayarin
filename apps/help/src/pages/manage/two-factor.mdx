---
title: Two-factor authentication
description: "Protect your account with an authenticator app."
---

Open **Settings** and find **Security**.

## Turn it on

1. Choose **Set up authenticator app**.
2. Scan the QR code with Google Authenticator or a similar app. Cannot scan? Open **Cannot scan? Enter the key by hand**.
3. Enter the 6-digit code the app shows, then **Verify and enable**.
4. **Save your recovery codes** (**Copy all**) somewhere safe. They are the way back in if you lose your phone.

Two-factor is only real once that code is confirmed.

## Other security actions

- **Change password** signs out every other device.
- **Login history** shows recent sign-ins. Unrecognised one? Change your password.
- Turning two-factor off asks you to confirm first.

Mayarin support never asks for your code or recovery codes.
