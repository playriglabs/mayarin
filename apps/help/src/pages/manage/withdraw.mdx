---
title: Withdraw and pay out
description: "Move money out of a managed wallet, or pay many people at once."
---

Both need the `wallets:withdraw` permission.

## Withdraw from a managed wallet

1. Open **Wallets**, then **Settlement balances**.
2. Choose to withdraw on the network that holds the balance.
3. Pick the **asset**, enter the **amount** and choose the **address** to send to.
4. Submit. You will see **Withdrawal submitted**.

You can only withdraw to a wallet you have **connected and verified**. If the button is disabled, it says **Connect and verify a wallet you own to withdraw to it.** See [set up your wallet](/start/set-up-wallet).

**Withdrawal history** lists every withdrawal, newest first.

## Pay many people (payouts)

Open **Payouts** to send a batch, for example to suppliers.

1. Under **New batch**, choose the network and where the money comes from.
2. Upload a CSV of recipients and amounts.
3. Review, then confirm. Your wallet may ask you to approve a token first when the allowance is short.

**Past batches** shows each one afterwards.
