---
title: A payment is stuck
description: "Find out why a payment is not moving and what, if anything, to do."
---

Open the payment under **Payments** and read the timeline. Find the status below.

| Status            | Usual cause                                           | What to do                                               |
| ----------------- | ----------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------- |
| `PAYMENT_PENDING` | The buyer has not paid, or the transfer has not confirmed yet. | Wait. Blockchains confirm in seconds to minutes. If the buyer says they paid, ask for the transaction hash. |
| `CLEARING` / `SETTLING` | Conversion or payout still running.            | Wait a few minutes.                                      |
| `FAILED` and buyer's money arrived | A step failed after funds came in. | Use **Retry settlement** on the payment. If it is refused, contact [support](/contact). |
| `FAILED` and no money arrived | Quote expired or buyer never paid. | The buyer starts a new payment.                          |

## Before you contact support

Have the payment id (`pi_…`), the buyer's transaction hash if you have it, and whether it is testnet or mainnet.

## Never

Do not ship goods for a payment that is not `SUCCESS`, and never ask the buyer to pay a second time while the first is still pending.
