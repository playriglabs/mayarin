title: Contact support
description: "How to reach the Mayarin team, and what to include so we can answer on the first reply."
---

Use the chat bubble in the dashboard, or the support widget on this site. Both reach the same inbox.

## Include this

- The **payment id** (`pi_…`) from the payment's page in the dashboard. It is the fastest way for us to find what happened.
- Whether you are on **testnet** or **mainnet**.
- What you expected, and what you saw instead. A screenshot helps.

## Never send

Your password, a one-time code from your authenticator app, or an `sk_` secret key. Support will never ask for them. If you pasted a key anywhere, [deactivate it](/integrate/api-keys#if-a-key-leaks) and make a new one.
