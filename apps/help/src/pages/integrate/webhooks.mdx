---
title: Webhooks
description: "Let your store or system hear about payments the moment they happen."
---

A webhook is a message Mayarin sends to a URL you own when something happens, such as a payment succeeding. Your order system should react to webhooks, never to the buyer returning to your site: a buyer who closes the tab is still paid.

Open **Webhooks** in the sidebar (needs `settings:manage`).

## Add an endpoint

1. In **Endpoints**, add the URL, for example `https://example.com/webhooks/mayarin`.
2. Copy its **signing secret**. Your server uses it to confirm a message really came from Mayarin.
3. Save the secret in your server configuration, not in page code.

## Check deliveries

**Deliveries** lists every attempt with its event and result. Search by delivery, event or endpoint id.

If your server does not answer with a `2xx`, Mayarin retries after about 1 minute, 5 minutes, 30 minutes, 2 hours and 12 hours, then marks it dead.

## Events you will see

- `payment.created`, `payment.state_changed`, `payment.failed`
- `payment.flagged` — see [flagged payments](/manage/flagged-payments)
- `escrow.disputed`, `escrow.dispute.message`, `escrow.dispute.resolved` — see [refunds and disputes](/manage/refunds-and-disputes)

Nothing arriving? See [webhooks not arriving](/troubleshooting/webhook-not-arriving). Writing the receiver? See [verify webhooks](https://docs.mayarin.xyz/guides/webhooks).
