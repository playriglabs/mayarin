---
title: Choose your networks and assets
description: "Decide which networks and assets buyers can pay you with."
---

In **Settings**, the networks section lists every network Mayarin supports and the assets each accepts.

1. Search or scroll to a network.
2. Turn on the assets you want to accept there.
3. Choose your **settlement asset** — the stablecoin you receive.
4. Choose **Save settings**. You will see **Settings saved.**

## Rules to know

- **Each network keeps at least one asset.** You cannot turn off the last one.
- A network always accepts the asset it settles in.
- **Cannot be priced** next to an asset means Mayarin has no live price for it yet, so buyers cannot pay with it.
- **Address unusable here** means your wallet address does not work on that network (for example an EVM address on Solana). Add an address for it first.
- The **Change history** list under Settings records every edit.

## Prices stay in your currency

You price in IDR, USD, SGD, THB or MYR. The buyer sees the amount in the asset they choose, locked for a short window. You are paid the amount you priced.
