---
title: Take your first payment
description: "Send yourself a test payment link on testnet and watch it settle."
---

Do this on **testnet** first.

1. Finish [wallet](/start/set-up-wallet) and [network](/start/choose-networks) setup.
2. Open **Payment links** and create a link. See [payment links](/accept/payment-links).
3. Open the link in another browser or window. This is what your buyer sees.
4. Pay it with a testnet wallet.
5. Back in the dashboard, open **Payments** and find it. The status moves to **SUCCESS** when you are credited. Statuses are explained in [payment statuses](/accept/payment-statuses).

## Check settlement

Open **Settlement**. **In flight** shows money on the way; **Settled** shows what landed; **Fees taken** shows what was deducted. See [settlement](/manage/settlement).

## Go live

Repeat the setup on https://dashboard.mayarin.xyz, switching any API key and base URL together. A testnet key does not work on mainnet.
