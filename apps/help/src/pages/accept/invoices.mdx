---
title: Invoices
description: "Bill a business client with a formal invoice they can pay on any supported asset."
---

Open **Invoices** in the sidebar (needs `catalog:manage`).

## Create and send

1. Choose to create an invoice.
2. Enter the client: company name, email, tax id and address as needed.
3. Add line items with a description and amount in your currency.
4. Add payment terms or a message, then **issue** it.

Mayarin emails the client a pay link when you gave an email. They pay with whatever asset they hold; you settle in your stablecoin.

## Good to know

- Your **city and country** print on every invoice. Set them in **Settings → Business profile** first.
- An issued invoice has a due date. Unpaid past it, it is shown as overdue.
- A paid invoice produces a receipt for the client.
- Recurring billing? Use [subscriptions](/accept/subscriptions), which issue one invoice per cycle for you.
