---
title: Payment links
description: "Create a link, share it anywhere, and get paid."
---

A payment link is a URL you send by chat, email or QR. The buyer opens it, picks an asset, and pays. Open **Payment links** in the sidebar. It needs the `catalog:manage` permission.

## Create one

1. Choose **Create link**.
2. Pick a **kind**:
   - **Fixed amount** — one set amount.
   - **Open amount (counter QR)** — the buyer types the amount, for a counter or a tip jar.
   - **From catalog** — pulls the price from a product in your [catalog](#sell-from-your-catalog).
3. Enter the amount in your own currency, for example `50.000,00` for IDR. Indonesian formatting groups thousands with `.` and marks decimals with `,`.
4. Optionally add a reference such as `order-1042` so you can match the payment to your own records.
5. Save, then copy the link or its QR code.

## Choose which assets buyers can use

Use **Edit accepted rails** on a link to narrow what a buyer can pay with. Leave it alone to accept everything you enabled in [Settings](/start/choose-networks).

## Sell from your catalog

Open **Catalog**, add products with a name and price, then create a **From catalog** link. Change the price in the catalog and new links follow it.

## After the buyer pays

The payment appears under **Payments**. If you set up [webhooks](/integrate/webhooks) your own system hears about it automatically. A link can be paid by different buyers, one payment each.
