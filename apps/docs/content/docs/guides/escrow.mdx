---
title: Buyer protection (escrow)
description: Hold a payment until the buyer confirms delivery, with rules that are fair to both sides.
---

With buyer protection, a payment does not go straight to your settlement wallet. It settles into an escrow address and moves on only when a condition holds:

| Who          | Can                                       | When                                   |
| ------------ | ----------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------- |
| The buyer    | Release the funds to you                  | Any time                               |
| Anyone       | Release the funds to you                  | After the hold, if nobody disputed     |
| You          | Refund the buyer                          | Any time                               |
| The buyer    | Open a dispute, stopping the auto-release | Before the hold ends                   |
| The arbiter  | Release, refund or split a disputed payment | Up to 7 days after the hold ends     |
| Anyone       | Refund a dispute nobody decided           | From 7 days after the hold ends        |

When the hold ends without a dispute, **Mayarin releases the funds to you automatically**, within about a minute. Mayarin pays the gas for that release; it cannot send the funds anywhere but to you. A dispute nobody decided is refunded to the buyer the same way.

The escrow is a smart contract with no owner, no admin and no upgrade. Its terms are fixed by its address, so nobody can change them after the buyer pays — Mayarin included.

## What keeps it fair

- **The hold is 1 to 90 days.** Long enough for the buyer to check the order, short enough that your money is not locked away.
- **A neutral arbiter.** Disputes go to **Mayarin Resolution** unless you name another arbiter. The arbiter can only send the funds to you or to the buyer. It can never be you or the buyer.
- **No dispute is stuck.** If the arbiter has not decided 7 days after the hold ends, the buyer is refunded — the same default as a card chargeback.
- **The buyer sees every term** — hold, arbiter, deadline, dispute bond — before they pay.
- **Disputing costs a bond, if you set one.** See [dispute bond](#dispute-bond).
- **Repeat disputers lose protection.** See [buyer standing](#buyer-standing).

## Turn it on for every payment

In the dashboard, open **Settings → Buyer protection**, tick it, and set the hold in days. Payment links, invoices and the hosted checkout then offer protection to the buyer.

The buyer **opts in by connecting a wallet** — the one they will release from. A buyer who pays from an exchange connects nothing and you are paid directly, as usual. Protection applies on every supported chain — Arbitrum Sepolia, Base Sepolia, Ethereum Sepolia and Arc testnet — and never on x402.

## Require it for one payment

Pass `escrow` when you create the intent. The buyer must then connect a wallet.

```ts
const intent = await mayarin.payment.createIntent({
  merchant: { id: "merchant_123", name: "Northwind Coffee", city: "Singapore", countryCode: "SG" },
  amount: { amount: "120.00", asset: "USD" },
  escrow: { holdSeconds: 7 * 24 * 60 * 60, bondBps: 500 },
});

const url = `https://pay.mayarin.xyz/checkout/pay/${intent.id}`;
```

`arbiter` (an address) and `bondBps` are optional; omitted, your defaults apply. Pass `escrow: null` to opt one payment out of your default.

## Watch the hold

```ts
const escrow = await mayarin.payment.getEscrow(intent.id);
// escrow.outcome: "held" | "released" | "refunded" | "split"
// escrow.disputed, escrow.terms.releaseAfter, escrow.terms.resolveBy, escrow.balance
// escrow.bond          — the dispute bond in settlement-token minor units, or null
// escrow.payerStanding — "good" | "flagged"
// escrow.dispute       — null, or { reference, status, openedAt, decidedAt, messages }
```

Amounts are decimal strings in the settlement token's minor units. The buyer sees the same state on their payment page, with **Release to merchant** and **Open a dispute**.

## When a buyer disputes

You are told at once:

- a webhook `escrow.disputed`, through the same signed, retried delivery as payment events — the event `id` is the dispute id. Each later step has its own event, `escrow.dispute.message` and `escrow.dispute.resolved`, and the dispute and its thread can be read and answered through the API: see [Disputes](/guides/disputes);
- an email to your admin accounts with the dispute number and a link to it;
- a notification in the dashboard.

```json
{
  "id": "dsp_01K…",
  "type": "escrow.disputed",
  "occurredAt": "2026-10-02T11:21:00.000Z",
  "data": {
    "disputeId": "dsp_01K…",
    "reference": "92965881664",
    "paymentIntentId": "pi_01M…",
    "chain": "base-sepolia",
    "payer": "0x1d96…f48f6",
    "resolveBy": "2026-10-10T11:21:00.000Z"
  }
}
```

The buyer states their case in the dispute's thread, from their payment page, and the arbiter answers there. You read the same thread in the dashboard under **Disputes**, or through [the disputes API](/guides/disputes) from your own system. Mayarin Resolution aims to decide within 3 days of the dispute.

## Dispute bond

Set a bond in **Settings → Buyer protection → Dispute bond**, 0–20% of what reaches the escrow, or per payment with `escrow.bondBps`. The buyer posts it, in the settlement token, when they dispute; checkout states it before they connect and asks them to approve it before disputing.

| Outcome                         | Payment              | Bond                     |
| ------------------------------- | -------------------- | ------------------------ |
| Released to you                 | You                  | You                      |
| Refunded to the buyer           | Buyer                | Buyer                    |
| Arbiter splits it               | Split by your share  | Whoever gets the larger share; the buyer at 50/50 |
| Nobody decided by the deadline  | Buyer                | Buyer                    |

A buyer who disputes and then releases has conceded, so the bond goes to you with the payment. A bond of zero disputes free.

## Buyer standing

A wallet that keeps disputing and keeps losing stops being offered buyer protection — with you and with every other merchant. When such a wallet connects, optional protection stays off and you are paid directly; a payment where you required protection is refused to it.

On an escrowed payment you see the buyer's standing, **Good** or **Flagged**, never their history with other merchants. A new wallet starts clean, so standing stops repeat abuse from one address; the bond is what makes abuse cost money.

## Refund

Open the payment in the dashboard and choose **Refund the payer**. Your settlement wallet sends it; for a Mayarin-managed Safe, connect its owner wallet. From code, send `escrow.calls.refund` to `escrow.factory` from your settlement address.

## Fees

Mayarin's fee is taken when the payment settles into escrow, so a refund returns the amount held: the payment minus the fee.

The escrow factory is `0xB14EEab74d84F900132632ff9dAcDe26C5043cad` on every supported chain. Escrows made before 2 October 2026 live under the first factory, `0xCB6dd0781D36FE95a5ff69248498E48909BB4e1B`, which has no bond and no split; `escrow.factory` always names the right one, so send calls there rather than to a hard-coded address.
